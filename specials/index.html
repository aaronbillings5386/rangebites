This page is where RangeBites would show specials that a restaurant has told us about directly. RangeBites does not invent deals, prices, or coupons, and it does not scrape them from other sites.

How a special gets listed. The email should include the restaurant’s name and address, what the special is, and when it runs. A special is only posted after a person reads it, and it comes down when it ends.

In the food finder. Separately, the Filters sheet can show promo text only when the OpenStreetMap listing itself already mentions a promo. That is not a live coupon feed, so confirm with the restaurant before you go.

Nothing is collected through this page. There is no diner mailing list and no account.
