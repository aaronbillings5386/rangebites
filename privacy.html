Privacy Policy

RangeBites · rangebites.com · Effective

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of RangeBites handles information. It is part of your agreement to use the Service, together with the Terms of Use.

1. Operator

RangeBites is a non-tracking food information website. It sends only the anonymous counts described in section 6. The Operator does not build a profile of you and does not save GPS.

1a. Non-tracking

RangeBites is an information-only food app at rangebites.com, designed, published, and operated by an individual website developer (the “Operator”). No account is required. The Operator does not run a diner email list. RangeBites is not a reservation service, not legal, tax, or financial advice, and not RangeBits or any finance product.

2. Information we do not collect

The Operator does not create user accounts, does not collect diner email addresses, does not use advertising trackers, and does not store a location history. The Operator does not store your latitude or longitude in cookies, local storage, or on the host as a profile.

3. Locate Me and city search

If you choose Locate Me, your device may share a coordinate with the browser so the Service can request nearby restaurants. That coordinate is held in memory for the current search. It is not saved as a GPS trail. In-memory coordinates are cleared when you tap Clear location, or when the page fully unloads. They are not wiped on a brief page hide, the iOS location permission sheet, or switching apps.

If you search a city, that text is sent to look up the area only when you tap Search or press Enter. There is no search-as-you-type. The city is not added to the page address. This device may remember range, filter choices, distance units (mi or km as text), and last city as text only, not coordinates. It may also store a flag that you tapped “Got it” on the first-search notice, and the counter flags described in section 6.

4. Saved restaurants (hearts)

If you heart a listing, this device may store its OpenStreetMap identifier, name, and address. That list does not include latitude or longitude and is not a GPS trail. Hearts remain on this device. You may unheart a restaurant or clear saved restaurants in Filters.

5. What is transmitted to provide the Service

To show places near you, your search goes through our host’s proxy to two OpenStreetMap services: the Overpass API instance run by OpenStreetMap France (overpass.openstreetmap.fr), which finds places, and the Nominatim geocoder run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), which looks up a city you type. If you use Locate Me, the place search includes the latitude and longitude your browser shared, rounded to 3 decimal places, which still places you within about 100 m. The host and those services also see your IP address. Map images load from a community OpenStreetMap tile server (tile.openstreetmap.de, run by FOSSGIS e.V. for OpenStreetMap Germany), which sees your IP address and the map area you view. We don’t save your coordinates or build a location history.

The host also sees your IP address on the counter requests (the page-open count and the Devices that found food count).

Restaurants may optionally submit an offer and a contact email on the Specials page for review. That is restaurant contact, not a diner mailing list.

The site is served over HTTPS. Files are hosted on here.now (Cloudflare).

6. Analytics and tracking

The Operator does not use Amplitude or similar product-analytics services to track you. The application does not log Locate Me, search, or deal taps as a user profile. The home page sends a first-party page-open count at most once per browser session. If the host retains it, that record is a kind label and a time. It does not include coordinates, name, or email. Page-open records are stored with our host and are not publicly readable.

“Devices that found food” count: the first time a search or Locate Me on this device shows food results, the Service sends one record that contains only the word “found” and the time. This device remembers that it was counted with a flag in its local storage, so it is counted once. No device code is sent. A random retry value may be sent in a request header for that one count. It stays only for this browser session, for at most 24 hours, and it is not a device identifier. These counter records (just that label plus a time) are stored with our host in a list that is publicly readable, so the number can be shown. They don’t include your name, email, location, or IP address. Anyone can add to that list, and clearing this site’s storage or using another browser can count a device again, so the number is approximate. Earlier versions of the site sent a random device code with this count; those older records are kept with our host and are no longer publicly readable.

We don’t track you across other websites, and we don’t let third parties do so through RangeBites, so we don’t change anything in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

7. Listings

Restaurant names, hours, and maps come from OpenStreetMap (some chain hours come from AllThePlaces store-locator data, CC0) as-is and may be wrong. Hours combine OpenStreetMap (ODbL) with AllThePlaces (CC0). The matching method is public in the GitHub repo: tools/build-atp-hours.py and app.js. There is no warranty that a listing is accurate, open, or still there.

8. What we do not do

The Operator does not sell personal information, does not require an account to browse, does not store reservations, does not use advertising trackers, and does not collect diner emails.

9. Third parties

Restaurant listings and hours come from OpenStreetMap contributors; some chain hours come from AllThePlaces (CC0), a public dataset built from chains’ own store locators. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy (Nominatim). Place searches are answered by OpenStreetMap France’s Overpass server, and map images by FOSSGIS e.V. (tile.openstreetmap.de); their own policies apply. When you open Google Maps, Apple Maps, or a restaurant website, those services’ policies apply. RangeBites does not scrape those services or display their star ratings on our pages.

10. Your choices

You may deny location permission and still search any city. You may unheart restaurants or clear saved restaurants in Filters, reset filters with Filters › Clear, and tap Forget next to “Last city” to remove the saved city on this device. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes everything the site stored on this device. Tap Clear location to wipe the in-memory search, or fully unload the page. A brief page hide, the iOS location permission sheet, or switching apps does not clear it.

11. Contact

Questions, corrections, accessibility problems, or legal notices: RangeBites (rangebites.com), contact: rangebites@agentmail.to. We don’t run a diner mailing list, and writing to us won’t add you to one.

12. Children

The Service is intended for persons 13 years of age and older. It is not directed to children under 13. The Operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13. Changes

The Operator may update this Policy by posting a revised version with a new effective date. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
