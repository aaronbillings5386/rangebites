      About & privacy

      RangeBites

      RangeBites helps you find food nearby. No account. Not a reservation. Not RangeBits. You must be 13 or older.

      Tap Locate Me or search any city on earth. Listings and hours come from OpenStreetMap and may be wrong or stale. Directions open in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Reviews open in Google Maps. We do not invent hours, star ratings, or coupons.

      Location

      Locate Me keeps your coordinate in memory for this search only. We never persist lat/lng. We do not save a location history. We do not store GPS on this device or on our host. This device may remember range, distance units (mi or km as text), and last city as text — not coordinates.

      Tap the heart to save or unheart a restaurant. Hearts on this device are OpenStreetMap id, name, and address — not a GPS trail. Heart a place again to unsave it. That list stays on this device only.

      To fetch restaurants, our host or proxy forwards the search to OpenStreetMap. The host sees that search and your IP. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap.

      We do not track you. Amplitude is off. The app does not log taps. Hits, if any, never include GPS.

      Deals

      A deal appears only if the listing already mentions a promo (e.g. description/note). We do not invent coupons. Copy listing text and confirm with the restaurant. It is not a live coupon feed and not a promise it will work at the register.
