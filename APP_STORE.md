## Disclosure body (clean)
RangeBites helps you find food nearby and see sample deals, then hand off to Maps or the merchant. Location powers a search, then it’s gone from our side — we don’t store it as a location history or profile, and don’t require an account for core use. Your browser may call public Overpass (OSM) and OpenStreetMap tiles (not CARTO). There is no RangeBites backend holding your location history. Web wipe: leave or close the page — not “delete the iOS app.”

Deals shown are illustrative / partner-ready — **not** live Honey scrapes — and may be wrong or expired. Always confirm with the restaurant. We don’t guarantee savings. We are not a delivery app. Reviews on Maps — we don’t store ratings and don’t invent stars. Local waitlist: `waitlist.html` (email only). Support address pending — do not invent one.

UI label: **Sponsored**. Disclosure: Sponsored pins are labeled. If you book or open a deal, we may earn a commission. You keep the discount. Pricing: We may earn a commission when you book or open a deal. You keep the full discount — we never skim the coupon. CTA: **Open deal**.
