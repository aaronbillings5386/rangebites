What it is

RangeBites helps you find food nearby. No account. Not a reservation. Not RangeBits. You must be 13 or older.

Tap Locate Me or search any city. Listings and hours come from OpenStreetMap (some chain hours come from AllThePlaces store-locator data, CC0) and may be wrong or stale. Hours combine OpenStreetMap (ODbL) with AllThePlaces (CC0). The matching method is public in the GitHub repo: tools/build-atp-hours.py and app.js. Directions open in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Reviews open in Google Maps. RangeBites does not invent hours, star ratings, or coupons.

Location

Locate Me keeps your coordinate in memory for this search only. We never persist lat/lng. We do not save a location history. This device may remember range, distance units, and last city as text — not coordinates.

To fetch restaurants, our host’s proxy sends the search to OpenStreetMap services: Overpass (run by OpenStreetMap France) and, for a city you type, Nominatim (run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation). With Locate Me, that search includes your coordinates rounded to 3 decimal places, which still places you within about 100 m. The host and those services see the search and your IP. Map images come from tile.openstreetmap.de (FOSSGIS e.V.), which sees your IP and the map area you view.

We do not track you. Amplitude is off. The app does not log taps. Opening the home page also sends a first-party page-open count, at most once per browser session: a kind label and a time. It does not include coordinates, name, or email. Devices that found food counts each device once, the first time a search or Locate Me shows food results. The site sends one record with just the word “found” and the time, and this device remembers it was counted. No device code is sent. A random retry value may be sent in a request header for that one count. It stays only for this browser session, for at most 24 hours, and it is not a device identifier. Anyone can add to that public list and clearing storage can count a device again, so it is approximate, not a count of people. Test traffic is excluded.

Tap the heart to save a restaurant name and address on this device. That list is not a GPS trail.
