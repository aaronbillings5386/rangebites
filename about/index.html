RangeBites

rangebites.com · Information-only food finder

What it is

RangeBites helps you find food nearby. No account. Not a reservation. Not RangeBits. You must be 13 or older.

Tap Locate Me or search any city. Listings and hours come from OpenStreetMap and may be wrong or stale. Directions open in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Reviews open in Google Maps. RangeBites does not invent hours, star ratings, or coupons.

Location

Locate Me keeps your coordinate in memory for this search only. We never persist lat/lng. We do not save a location history. This device may remember range, distance units, and last city as text — not coordinates.

We do not track you. Amplitude is off. The app does not log taps. Page opens is a first-party page-open count (kind and time only). It is not unique visitors and may undercount.

Tap the heart to save a restaurant name and address on this device. That list is not a GPS trail.
