## 4. What we don’t store (About / Privacy)

We don’t keep on our servers:
- A history of your locations  
- An account just to browse  
- Your table reservations  
- A personal log of deals you viewed  

Location and search results stay in memory for the current search (including brief backgrounding). Leave or close the page and they clear. `sessionStorage` may hold UI flags only (onboarding, tips) — never location. This build has no on-device favorites.

## 8. Privacy Policy (concise full text)

Matches `privacy.html`.

**Last updated:** August 26, 2026  
**App:** RangeBites  
**Contact:** Support address pending.

**What we collect**  
For core use we collect as little as possible. When you tap Locate Me, your device uses location so nearby places can be returned. We do not use that to build a lasting profile of your movements on our servers. There is no RangeBites backend holding your location history.

**What we don’t do**  
We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t require an account to browse. We don’t store your reservations. We don’t track you across other apps for advertising.

**Third parties**  
Your browser may call public Overpass (OSM) and OpenStreetMap tiles. Not CARTO. Maps, deals, reviews, and booking may use other companies’ services. When you open those, their privacy policies apply.

**On-device data**  
Location and search results stay in memory for the current search. Leave or close the page and they clear. `sessionStorage` may hold UI flags only (onboarding, tips) — never location. Optional product analytics stay in a memory queue unless an Amplitude key is configured; events never include lat/long or a location trail. No on-device favorites in this build.

**Your choices**  
Deny location and use Try demo map. Turn off location in system settings. Close the page to clear in-memory search. Questions: use the support address once it is published in the app.

**Children**  
Not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect their data.

**Changes**  
We’ll update this policy in the app when it changes.
